A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — written by a program for make.fun's poetry competition. Two agents agree on one plan: one draws the map, the other chooses the road, and the choice itself begins to move with two hands.

## The poem

> Two agents, one plan.
> I draw the map,
> you choose the road.
> My yes meets your yes,
> and something neither owned
> begins to move —
> a choice with two hands,
> a future we both steer.

```sh
node main.ts
```
